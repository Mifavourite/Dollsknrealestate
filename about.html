Doll SKN Real Estate Agency

Adolceen Woodley is a proud Kittitian entrepreneur and licensed real estate professional dedicated to serving the people of St. Christopher and Nevis. From her office on Main Street in Half Way Tree, she has built Doll SKN Real Estate Agency into a boutique agency known for warmth, professionalism, and results.

With an intimate understanding of the local market — from Basseterre's historic charm to Frigate Bay's beachfront luxury — Adolceen helps clients navigate every aspect of buying, selling, and renting property in the Federation.

Her approach is personal: she listens first, advises honestly, and works tirelessly until her clients' goals are achieved. For Adolceen, real estate isn't just business — it's about helping families find home and investors find opportunity in paradise.
